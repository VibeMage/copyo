# Paster App Icon Style Breakthrough Exploration

## Goal

Explore icon concepts that feel surprising and memorable rather than merely competent. Produce two autonomous rounds with four independent styles per round: anime, modern, science fiction, and cyberpunk. Each style receives its own graphic metaphor instead of reusing the existing stacked-clipboard composition.

The product signal remains fast recall of mixed clipboard content such as text, images, links, and files. The exploration may express that signal symbolically rather than literally. These are comparison candidates only and do not replace the current AppIcon.

## Approach

Use a hybrid of product meaning and stylistic reinvention. Every candidate must have:

- one dominant silhouette readable at 32 × 32;
- one clear focal idea rather than a collection of decorative effects;
- some visual cue for stored, recalled, or mixed-format information;
- a clean full-square background with no simulated transparency checkerboard;
- no readable text, keyboard glyphs, watermark, presentation mockup, or external scene.

Generate all eight candidates independently with built-in ImageGen and without ip-as-logo or earlier icon images as references. Preserve each first returned concept without silent creative retries.

## Round 7 — Product-Grounded Impact

### R7-A — Anime Data Familiar

A cel-shaded floating memory charm shaped like a compact magical card familiar. It holds three large content fragments: a clean line block, a blue image tile, and a small link-like ribbon. Use an energetic diagonal silhouette, crisp ink-like contour accents, saturated sky blue, coral, and cream on a deep navy background. Keep it icon-like rather than a full character scene.

### R7-M — Modern Kinetic Fold

A bold contemporary symbol made from one folded ribbon that becomes three overlapping content planes. Use decisive negative space, asymmetric balance, matte cobalt, vermilion, and warm white on graphite. The result should feel like a premium design-tool icon, not a document or checklist illustration.

### R7-S — Sci-Fi Memory Prism

A luminous data prism with three suspended internal layers representing text, image, and file content. Use cool cyan, ultraviolet, and controlled pearlescent highlights on near-black blue. Keep the prism broad and readable, with restrained holographic depth and no tiny interface markings.

### R7-C — Cyberpunk Neon Cache

A compact neon cache module: an angular frame enclosing three bold content shards, one of which is unmistakably image-like. Use electric cyan, hot magenta, and a small acid-yellow accent on black-violet. Employ selective glow and one strong glitch cut, avoiding dense circuitry and illegible micro-detail.

## Round 8 — Experimental Brand Leap

### R8-A — Anime Memory Grimoire

A cel-shaded magical memory book bursting open into three large floating content talismans: image, text block, and color swatch. Use dramatic foreshortening, a star-shaped highlight, indigo, mint, and warm peach. It should feel collectible and expressive without becoming a full illustration scene.

### R8-M — Modern Negative-Space Portal

A minimal sculptural portal formed by two thick interlocking shapes; its negative space suggests a captured content card being instantly recalled. Use polished but mostly flat geometry, black cherry, electric orange, and off-white. Prioritize an ownable silhouette over literal clipboard imagery.

### R8-S — Orbital Recall Core

A glowing central memory core with three broad content tiles locked into a clean orbital path. Use a dark cosmic base, cyan-white core, violet orbit, and one warm amber data tile. The orbit must read as instant recall rather than cloud synchronization.

### R8-C — Cyberpunk Memory Chip

A bold chromed memory chip with three inset content cells: image, lines, and color field. Use black chrome, neon cyan, fuchsia, and acid lime with a single diagonal energy slash. Keep the outer silhouette simple and avoid realistic PCB complexity.

## Delivery

Save Round 7 and Round 8 in separate project-local directories under the dated icon exploration folder. Record every exact prompt, source path, dimensions, color mode, and provider in a generation report.

Present the eight candidates in a single visual comparison grouped into four style cards. Each style card shows its Round 7 and Round 8 candidates side by side with 32-pixel previews. The user can select or reference any full label such as R7-S or R8-M.

Do not modify art/icon-master.png or Paster/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset during this exploration.
