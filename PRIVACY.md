# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Effective date / 生效日期: 2026-09-01**

## English

Paster does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. Period.

- **Clipboard history** is stored only on your Mac (and, if you enable sync, in a
  folder you choose — such as your own iCloud Drive). It never touches our servers,
  because we don't have any.
- **No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no network requests.**
- **Concealed content** (e.g. from password managers) is never recorded.
- The optional sync feature reads and writes files only in the folder you explicitly
  select. Data in that folder is governed by the storage provider you chose
  (e.g. Apple iCloud).

Since no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, share, or delete.
Deleting the app (and its data folder) removes everything.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/VibeMage/Paster/issues

## 中文

Paster 不收集、不传输、不出售任何数据。

- **剪贴板历史**只保存在你的 Mac 上（若开启同步，则保存在你自己选择的文件夹中，
  例如你自己的 iCloud Drive）。它不会经过我们的服务器——因为我们根本没有服务器。
- **无统计分析、无遥测、无崩溃上报、无任何网络请求。**
- 密码管理器等标记为隐藏的内容**从不记录**。
- 可选的同步功能只读写你明确选择的文件夹；该文件夹中数据的处理方式由你选择的
  存储服务商（如 Apple iCloud）决定。

由于不收集任何数据，我们无从访问、共享或删除你的数据。
删除应用（及其数据目录）即可清除一切。

如有疑问：https://github.com/VibeMage/Paster/issues
